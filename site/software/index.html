Benchmarked, not vibes

Best in her weight class — and honest about the ceiling above it.

MoltBench scores wire-to-report work: grounding, attribution, and adjudication of deliberately contaminated bundles, under blind judges. Pepper's first model failed it — that failure became the release gate. Her current desk model cleared it: +23 grounding points over its own untuned base, 10/12 blind wins against Qwen3-4B-2507, 11/12 against Qwen2.5-7B. Models 6× and 13× her size still out-broadcast her — 10/12 and 11/12 — and those rows are published too, because a leaderboard that hides its ceiling isn't one. The judge column is the claim; regex grounding is only the screening metric (it rewards terseness — a lesson this benchmark learned in public). Protocol and bundles →
